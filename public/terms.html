4. Fair play

Wiki-Guesser is a game about knowing things quickly. Please don't:

  • Use bots, scripts, macros, or automated tooling to play, queue, or guess.
  • Manipulate ratings — arranging wins with a friend, running multiple accounts against yourself, or deliberately losing.
  • Exploit bugs rather than reporting them. If you find something broken, tell us and we'll be grateful.
  • Interfere with the service: flooding it with requests, probing it, or trying to reach data that isn't yours.
  • Scrape or bulk-collect data from the site, including the leaderboard.

We may adjust, reset, or remove ratings and match records that we reasonably believe were obtained by any of the above, and we may suspend accounts that keep doing it.

5. Chat and conduct

Rooms have a chat box. Don't use it to harass, threaten, or abuse other players; to post hateful content, sexual content involving minors, personal information about anyone, or spam and advertising; or to do anything illegal.

We do not store chat messages — they pass through our server to the other players in the room and are not written to disk. One practical consequence: we cannot retrieve a conversation after the fact.

Reporting. Every message has a report button. Using it sends us that one message, along with who sent it and who reported it, so that a human can look at it — this is the only way a chat message reaches us, and it is the most reliable way to tell us about a problem. You can also email wiki.guesser.online@gmail.com, but do it while you still remember what was said, and take a screenshot if you can. Don't file reports in bad faith; doing so is itself a misuse of the service.

If you'd rather not see chat at all, you can switch it off for yourself in your profile, and it stays off in future games. If you host a private room, you can switch chat off for everyone in it.

We review reports when we can. We're a small operation and can't promise a response time, or that we will act on every report.

Whatever you type is yours, and you keep it. By sending it you give us permission to relay it to the other players in your room, which is the only thing we do with it.
