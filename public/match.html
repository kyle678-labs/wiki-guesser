How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same nine articles, shuffled the same way. They change at midnight UTC.
  2. Every caption starts under the wrong picture — none of them is right for free.
  3. Tap one caption, then another to swap the two. Tap the same one again to change your mind.
  4. You're on the clock. It starts the moment the grid appears and stops when all nine are right, and that time is your score — fastest wins.
  5. We won't tell you which ones are right, only when all nine are. You can guess your way there, but every wrong arrangement costs you time.
  6. Swaps don't score, but par — the fewest a grid can be sorted in — is worth chasing anyway: the short way round is usually the quick one. One attempt per day.
