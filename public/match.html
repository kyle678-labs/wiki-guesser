How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same nine articles, shuffled the same way. They change at midnight UTC.
  2. Every caption starts under the wrong picture — none of them is right for free.
  3. Tap one caption, then another to swap the two. Tap the same one again to change your mind.
  4. Each swap counts. Your score is how many it took, so lower is better — par is the fewest possible.
  5. We won't tell you which ones are right, only when all nine are. You can guess your way there, but every wrong arrangement costs you swaps.
  6. Nothing is timed — take as long as you like. Equal scores are ranked by who got there first. One attempt per day.
