How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same article each day. It changes at midnight UTC.
  2. You start with the first four words of its opening — with the article's own name blanked out.
  3. Guess wrong and one more word appears. Your score is how many words it took, so lower is better.
  4. Near-misses count: a typo or a missing accent will still be accepted.
  5. One attempt per day, and the clock starts when the puzzle loads.
