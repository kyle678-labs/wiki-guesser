How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same article each day. It changes at midnight UTC.
  2. You start with the first six words of its opening — with the article's own name blanked out.
  3. The boxes above the input are the answer's shape: how many words it has, and how long each one is.
  4. Every guess is marked letter by letter. A right letter, right place · B in the answer, elsewhere · C not in it. Letters you place correctly stay filled in above.
  5. Guess wrong and one more word appears. Your score is how many guesses it took, so lower is better.
  6. Near-misses count: a typo or a missing accent will still be accepted.
  7. Nothing is timed — take as long as you like. Equal scores are ranked by who got there first.
  8. One attempt per day.
