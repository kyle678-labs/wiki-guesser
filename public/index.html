How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same clue pulled live from a Wikipedia article — either its picture or its opening description with the topic blanked out.
  2. Type your guess before the timer runs out — it's hidden from opponents.
  3. Naming the topic scores big. Can't name it? Any word that appears in its article still scores — the more often it appears, the more points.
  4. Answer fast: a speed bonus is added on top of a scoring guess and decays as the timer runs down, so the quicker of two matching guesses wins the round.
  5. Highest total after all rounds wins. In ranked, your Elo moves accordingly.
