How it works

  1. Everyone gets the same picture, cut the same way, scrambled the same way. It changes at midnight UTC.
  2. Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
  3. Drag a tile onto another to swap the two. On a keyboard, the arrow keys swap the focused tile with its neighbour.
  4. Each turn and each swap is one move. Your score is how many moves it took, so lower is better — par is the fewest possible.
  5. Nothing is timed — take as long as you like. Equal scores are ranked by who got there first.
  6. We only tell you what the picture was once you've finished it. One attempt per day.
